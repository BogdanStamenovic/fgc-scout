Entry rules: confirm for every teammate

  • Serbia is not on Korea's K-ETA exemption list (the 2026 exemption covers 22 countries, Serbia not among them). Serbians are visa-free for 90 days, but anyone aged 18–64 needs an approved K-ETA: k-eta.go.kr, ₩10,000. Usually approved in minutes, can take up to 72 h. Airlines check it at check-in, so if you boarded, yours is likely fine.
  • 17 or younger: exempt from K-ETA, but must submit the free e-Arrival Card online (the 3 days before arrival; paper cards ended Dec 2025). Doable from a phone, even at the gate or on landing.

Sources: LoyaltyLobby (22-country list), MOFA, epickor.

The 3am arrival

  • First ask the mentors / FIRST Global whether a pickup is arranged. Nothing about transfers is on their public page.
  • No train, no night bus to Songdo. AREX starts about 05:14, the 6777 limousine about 05:00, and the airport's night buses serve only Seoul and Gyeonggi.
  • Take a taxi from the Incheon taxi stand: T1 pickup 5D, T2 3D. Uber and Kakao T also work.
  • Fare: daytime about ₩25–30k, ~30 min, plus the Incheon Bridge toll (₩2,000). Night surcharge is +20% from 02:00–04:00. Estimate: ₩32–40k per car (€21–26), split 4 ways. A big group with luggage may need a jumbo or call van.
  • Registration opens at 08:30. Sleep. Lunch and dinner are provided to official team members in the Grand Ballroom, so your own spending is mostly snacks and free evenings.

Plan by free slot

The robot stays in the pit every night. Your real free time is evenings, plus Saturday night and maybe Sunday.

Songdo Central Park

Free, open 24 h. Water taxi ₩4,000 (West boathouse to 19:00 on weekdays, East to 21:00). Walk to the Tri-Bowl and the Hanok Village, then BBQ near Central Park station. Optional: the G-Tower 33F observatory, free with ID check hours.

Afterwards: chimaek, or a convenience-store run near the hotel.

THU 8 OCT · after 17:30

Triple Street + Hyundai Premium Outlet

Technopark station, exit 2. An Apple reseller on B1 (earphones), the NC Cube Canal Walk, a cinema, and the B1 food court.

FRI 9 OCT · Hangul Day · after 17:00

Incheon Chinatown → Fairy Tale Village → Wolmido

About 57 min by subway (long transfer walk at Woninjae); a taxi split 4 ways is faster. Jjajangmyeon for dinner where it was invented, then the murals. Wolmi Sea Train ₩8,000 (₩6,000 for ages 13–18), runs to 21:00 Fri–Sun. Rides at Wolmido ₩3–6k each.

Alternative: Gangbyeon Techno Mart (open to 21:00 on holidays), but it's ~2 h each way. Too tight.

SAT 10 OCT · after 18:00

Celebrate in Songdo, or go to Hongdae

Hongdae busking peaks 20:00–22:00 on Saturdays. PC bang ₩1,000–1,800/h, coin karaoke ~₩7,000/h. Under 19: no PC bang or noraebang from 22:00 to 09:00, and staff check passports. Last M6724 bus from Sinchon back to Songdo leaves 00:15.

SUN 11 OCT · if you're still here

A full Seoul day

  • Morning: Gyeongbokgung (09:00–18:00, ₩3,000), then Bukchon. Its red zone is open to tourists 10:00–17:00 only; outside those hours the fine is ₩100,000.
  • Afternoon, tech: Gangbyeon Techno Mart (Sun 10:30–21:00) or Yongsan Etland (10:30–20:00). Seonin Plaza is probably shut on Sundays.
  • Evening: Myeongdong street food, then N Seoul Tower (to 23:00, ₩29,000). Or COEX Starfield Library (free, to 22:00), or Lotte World Tower Seoul Sky (~₩27k+, foreigner discounts).
Tech sights that don't fit

Samsung Innovation Museum (Suwon) is free, but weekdays are by reservation 13:00–14:00 only, Saturday is walk-in, and it's closed Sundays and holidays, including Fri 9 Oct. Samsung d'light is permanently closed; the Samsung Gangnam flagship at Gangnam Station replaced it check hours.

Tech shopping

Read first
Korea isn't cheap for electronics right now, and Serbian customs flips the maths. DRAM prices doubled between March and September. Samsung raised Galaxy S26 prices in Korea on 1 Oct (+₩149,600). And the Serbian duty-free allowance is only €100 per person (no higher air limit; €430 is the EU's). Above that, expect roughly 10% duty + 20% VAT ≈ +32% on the whole value secondary sources.

Korean-model phones ("N" Galaxy)

  • eSIM is unclear for Korean S25/S26 variants. In the store, check Settings → Connections → SIM manager → Add eSIM before paying.
  • Bands: the S25 Korean model reportedly has LTE B20 + B28, which matter in Serbia. Check the exact model number.
  • Forced camera shutter sound unverified. Warranty is effectively regional.

Earphones

Apple: Apple Stores in Seoul, or the reseller on B1 of Hyundai Premium Outlet Songdo (Technopark exit 2). Other brands: Samsung stores, Lotte Hi-Mart, Electroland.

Tourist tax refund

  • Minimum ₩15,000 per receipt. Refunded instantly at the till if the receipt is under ₩1,000,000 (₩5M cap per trip); above that, claim at the airport.
  • You get back ~5–8%, not the full 10%. Bring your passport. Small PC shops often aren't tax-free registered.
  • Keep refunded goods in your carry-on until airport customs has seen them.

Food

How ordering works: a kiosk by the door (English mode is common), a table tablet, or the call bell on the table. Don't wave. No tipping.

Eat at least once

Samgyeopsal or galbi BBQ (usually a 2-portion minimum) · chimaek (fried chicken + beer) · tteokbokki · kimbap · bibimbap · jjajangmyeon · a convenience-store run: triangle kimbap, ramyeon with the in-store hot water, banana milk.

Worth the trip

  • Incheon Chinatown: jjajangmyeon ₩7–9k, tangsuyuk ₩15–20k (Sinseung Banjeom, Gonghwachun). The Jjajangmyeon Museum is ₩1,000.
  • Gwangjang Market (Seoul): bindaetteok ₩5,000, mayak kimbap ₩4–8k. Food stalls 09:00–23:00.
  • Myeongdong street food ~16:00–23:00, ₩4–8k per item. Touristy, about 2× normal prices.

Budget: convenience-store meal ₩5–8k · casual meal ₩9–15k · BBQ ₩20–30k per person.

Drinking age is 19 by birth year: born in 2007 or earlier is fine in 2026.

Getting around

  • Maps: use Naver Map or Kakao Map; Google Maps is weak here. Taxis: Kakao T (takes foreign Visa/MC; if 3-D Secure blocks the card, choose "pay to driver"), k.ride, or Uber.
  • T-money card (~₩4,000) at any convenience store; top up with cash. Incheon Line 1 base fare ₩1,550.
  • Venue station: Incheon National University (Incheon Line 1). Central Park station is ~15 min on foot.

Last ride
Leave central Seoul by 23:00. The last M6724 from Sinchon is 00:15. The last Line 1 toward Incheon from Seoul Station is ~23:30, and the last Incheon L1 from Bupyeong to Songdo ~00:50 check on Naver Map. A late taxi back is ~₩60–90k.

Practical

  • Weather: clear and dry. ~22–25 °C by day, 11–13 °C at night. Bring a light jacket for the waterfront.
  • Plugs: Type C/F, 220 V. Serbian plugs fit with no adapter.
  • Money: cards (Revolut/Wise too) work almost everywhere. Carry some cash for markets and Yongsan. Woori/KB "Global ATM" charge ~₩2–3k. Always pay in KRW and refuse the ATM's own conversion.
  • LG U+ eSIM: data-only plans can't receive SMS, and some Korean apps need SMS to sign up. Some "unlimited" plans slow to ~5 Mbps after 3 GB/day. Hotspot is usually allowed, so share it with teammates. Turn off roaming on the Serbian SIM.
  • Fri 9 Oct is Hangul Day: banks and offices close; shops and transit run.
  • Emergency: 112 police · 119 ambulance · 1330 tourist helpline. FIRST Global has a medic station at the venue.
